Where it works — and where it does not

Skill above climatology compares the model against simply predicting the average profile for that location, which needs no satellite data at all. Positive means the surface observations genuinely add information. Below about 300 m it turns negative: the surface stops constraining temperature that deep, so we do not claim skill there. That is physics, not a defect — and it is the range that matters operationally (cyclone heat content and the thermocline both sit in the upper 200 m).
Trained on GLORYS reanalysis, evaluated on days never seen in training. Every value shown is precomputed — the page runs no model and needs no server.
