At a glance

Architecture

Every depth is produced by its own learned query attending over one shared 128-dimensional latent. The 15 levels are not 15 independent regressions — they are one physically continuous water column, and sharing the encoder is what keeps the profile smooth and correctly ordered.

What the model actually depends on

Each input is blanked in turn and the model re-run. The useful reading is not that accuracy drops — of course it does — but which variable it drops for. A channel whose removal costs nothing was never being used. A negative cost means removing it slightly helped: that channel is contributing noise, not signal.

Accuracy by depth

Skill vs climatology is the honest metric: climatology is the per-location mean profile, which needs no satellite data at all. Beating a baseline is not impressive if neither beats “the usual temperature here”.
